Offensive Zone Positioning

Creating triangular support structures gives the puck carrier multiple passing options and creates depth in the offensive zone.

Options

There are many options that feed off of the triangle set up.

If you do not have the puck, move around and give the puck carrier more options.


The videos are not meant for mastering the play, it is to show you how to move to open space.
