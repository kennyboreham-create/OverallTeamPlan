Breakouts

When At First You Don't Succeed, Try Again

Regrouping is when a team pulls back, resets its formation, and rebuilds its attack after moving the puck backward to maintain control.

Hockey is a game of options, if your team has Possession and you do not have the puck. Get open for a pass. The more options the puck carrier has the higher success rate.

What is regrouping?

The other team can't score when you have possession

Execution

Dump And Chase

There are times to dump and chase, but not all the time

If it is late in a shift and you need a change then dump and chase is the go to move. If you have tried breaking in the zone but it just isn't happening this shift then dump and chase is a way to change momentum. Otherwise keep possession by Regrouping instead.





Keeping Possession Is A Good Thing

It is ok to try again

Regroup, move the puck back to the defense and be patient. Forwards try and time the pass from the defensemen when swinging around.





Know When To Regroup

There is a time for everything
