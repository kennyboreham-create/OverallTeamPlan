Breakouts

The Game Of Options

The breakout is when we have possession and are trying to bring the puck out of our zone.

Hockey is a game of options, if your team has Possession and you do not have the puck. Get open for a pass. The more options the puck carrier has the higher success rate.

Watch key breakout concepts in motion.

Breakout Concepts & Execution

D to D (Over-Breakout)

Use this breakout to spread players out from the strongside

Pass the puck over to your D partner (Always Talking). After that there are options. He can skate it out if no one is him, pass to a forward that is swinging over, look up ice, or perform a pass off of the boards to put the puck in open space.





Rim

Use this when you are out of other options

This should be the last option because it usually goes to a board battle. The D fires the puck along the boards so the winger can get it out. Keep in mind the weakside winger could be in front of the net if the puck was just won. Always talk to each other.





Wheel

Use this when there is no defender on you

If no on is on you, skate it out. Make sure all other players follow the puck carrier fast to create an odd man chance.





Quick Up

Use this if you see a good quick option

If you are a winger and your D has the puck. Think about getting open. If you are open at the right time then you will get a pass.
